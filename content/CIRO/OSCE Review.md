# Systemic Conditions

Hyperpituitarism

Hypothryroidism - weight gain, hair-thinning, big tongue, deepening voice, dry skin

Hyperthyroidism - exopthalmous

Addison's Disease
- Hyperpigmentation - skin and oral
- Oral Manifestation: Hyperpigmentation
- Deficient Cortisol & Glucocorticoid

Cushing's Syndrome
- Too much Cortisol

Gardner's Syndrome
- On panorex: Multiple supernumerary teeth (clavicles present)
- intestinal polyps
- DD: Cleidocranial dysplasia (clavicles absent or hypoplastic/partially formed)

Leukemia - inflamed gingiva; leukocytosis (high WBC count; normal 4500-10,000)
