# EXAM

** CENTRIC RELATION**
- definition: a cranio-mandibular position that involves healthy TMJs; a working position (not relaxed), a physiological position, sound free position, pain free position, where the condyle is in the most anterior (away from bilaminar zone which has blood and nerves), superior and medial (distance of medial wall and medial surface of condyle should be same in both condyles) position in the glenoid fossa (3 dimension), apex of condyle should be touching posterior part of articular disc (disc is 3mm thick), disc is touching deepest part of glenoid fossa, anterior convexity of the condyle is touching the intermediate zone of the articular disc (disc is 1mm thick in this area), is touching the posterior convexity of the articular eminence; occlusion should not interfere with this position, meaning the occlusion should always be in centric relation, if there is any occlusal interference, the condyle is not in centric relation

**Signs and Symptoms of not being in CR**
Symptoms (something you cannot measure) - said by the patient
- tenderness, muscular pain, intracapsular pain, synovitis, capsulitis, tinnitus, pain from a tooth or group of teeth, clicking/popping/crepitus sounds, 

Signs (something you can measure) - absence of canine/anterior guidance, premature contacts, open contacts, posterior crossbite, class ii/iii maloclussion, mandibular deviation during opening/closing

If patient has any sign/symptom of occlusal dysfunction, then the patient must undergo an occlusal splint therapy between orthodontics, prosthodontics or surgical procedures
if patient has any sign/symptom of TMD, the patient must undergo occlusal splint therapy before orthodontics, prosthodontics or surgery.
**symptom -** something you cannot evaluate/measure; clicking, pain, sounds, tenderness, pain in one tooth, pain in one tooth or group of teeth, sensibility
**clinical sign** - something you can measure; mm, degrees, etc - absence of canine/anterior guidance, premature guidance, open contacts, posterior crossbite, class ii/iii maloclussion, 
signs of tmd - clicking, crepitation, hypermobiliy, hypomobility, deflective movements, osteoarthritis of condyle or articular eminence, osteoarthrosis, burping lesions (osteophytic lesions), decortication of condyles
symptoms of tmd - muscular pain, intracapsular pain, synovitis, capsulitis, tinnitus

BRACHIOFACIAL
- shorter face

- horizontal growth - mandible grows inwards to towards the cranium
