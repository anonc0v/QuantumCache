# Zirconia Restoration
Crown:
- sandblast + Alcohol + ceramic primer
Tooth:
- Etch
- Tooth Primer: 20s + Panavia V Cement

## Metal-Porcelain Restoration
Crown:
- sandblast + alcohol + ceramic Primer
Tooth:
- Tooth Primer 20sec + Panavia V Cement

### Lithium Disilicate
Crown:
- sandblast + alcohol + Porcelain etch + Rinse & Dry + Ceramic Primer
 Tooth:
- Chlorhexidine + Rinse & Dry + Etch + on Composite area: silane/monobond + Tooth Primer 20s + Panavia V5 cement

#### Fiberglass Post
Post: 
Alcohol 1 Min Dry + Silane + Adhesive (Optibond FL) + dual cure, self-cure cement

# Immediate Dentin Sealing (IDS)
- Advantages on indirect partial restorations (i.e. crown abutments after prep and before impression taking)
	- 1) Reduces bacterial microleakage
	- 2) Reduces Hypersensitivity
	- 3) Improves Bonding Quality

# Gold Standard Adhesive Systems
4th generation (**total etch**) -  OptiBond FL (Kerr) 
6th generation (**selective etch**) - Clearfil SE (Kuraray)

# Classification of adhesive bonding systems 

![[Prostho classification of bonding systems.png]]
4th generation: 3-step: Etch + Primer + Bonding
5th generation: 2-step: Etch + Self-priming adhesive
6th generation: 2-step: Self-etching primer + Bonding
7th generation: Single Step - Mix Bottle A and Bottle B

![[Prostho Classification of Adhesive Bonding systems 2.jpg]]
4th generation: Total Etch (E&R) - 3 steps: etch / primer / bonding (OptibondFL)
5th generation: Total Etch (E&R) - 2 steps: etch / primer + bonding 
6th generation: Selective Etch - 2 steps: etch + Primer / bonding (Clearfil SE)
7th generation: Selective Etch - 1 step: Etch + Primer + bonding
Universal: 
- 1 step (SE)
Etch + primer + bonding 
- 2 step (E&R and Total Etch)
Etch / Primer + bonding; 
Enamel Etch/Dentine etch + Primer + bonding

# Conditioning Sclerotic Dentin
1) EDTA
2) Phosphoric Acid

# Thickness of retraction cord considerations:
1) width of sulcus
2) level of finishing line
3) health of periodontium
4) amount of fluid present

# Function of retraction cord
1st cord: thinner, deeper - Retract gingiva + prevent contamination of margins by crevicular fluid/blood 
2nd cord: bigger - Expand the sulcus

# Fase V
1) Cleaning
2) Periodic Xrays
3) Check Margin every appointment
4) Polishing of restorations
