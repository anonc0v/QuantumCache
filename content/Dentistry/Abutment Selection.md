# Abutment Selection
1) Crown:root ratio: optimum 2:3; acceptable: 1:1
2) Nature of antagonist?
3) Root configuration: roots broader labiolingually than mesiodistally are preferable to roots that are round in cross-section; multirooted teeth with diverging roots offer better periodontal support than single rooted teeth or teeth with multi-rooted teeth that converge or fuse; 
4) Periodontal ligament area - more root surface area will offer more support; Ante’s Law - root surface area of the abutment teeth had to equal or surpass that of the teeth being replaced wtih pontics
5) Periodontal Health - clinically healthy gingiva, no inflammation, no periodontitis; Mobility - not necessarily contraindicated but there may be a need to splint if extra support is needed
6) position/ or function of tooth in occlusion
