# Why VANGUARD?
- client-owned
- operates at cost
	- **lower expense ratios; average 0.18%**
- I invest in the mutual funds, not Vanguard itself
- the mutual funds are a separate entity; if Vanguard ceases to exist, your mutual funds will still exist.

## I get paid by
- the returns generated by the mutual funds

# versus others:
- family business (Fidelity)
- publicly traded and owned by shareholders (T. Rowe Price)

## Owner gets paid by
- returns from operating individual mutual funds

## I get paid by
- The remaining profits after deducting the owner's returns and the cost of operating the investment firm (salaries, supplies, rent, etc)
- in summary, you are paid only after deducting the operational costs of your fund and the profit that goes to the owners

### Result?
- **Higher expense ratios - Avg of 1.01%** vs Vanguard's avg of 0.18%
