# Lithium Disilicate
- LS2 
- Classification: glass-ceramic - a class of particle-filled glass material
- created in the 1990s - named "IPS Empress 2" (Ivoclar Vivadent, Schaan, Liechtenstein)
- composition: 65% vol% lithium disilicate, small needle-shaped crystals embedded in a glass matrix, 1 vol% porosity
- Mechanical characteristics: flexural strength: 350 MPa; fracture toughness (KIC): 3.3 MPa√m; heat extrusion temperature: 920 °C; thermal expansion co-efficient (CTE): 10.6 + 0.25 ppm/°C)
- 2005 - New LS2 is called "IPS e.max press"
- better mechanical characteristics: flexural strength: 370–460 MPa; fracture toughness (KIC): 2.8–3.5 MPa√m)
- one side has a layered, tightly interlocked distribution of elongated disilicate crystals, which hinders crack propogation, and the other side has a mismatch of thermal expansion coefficients of LS2 crystals and glassy matrix, so that the LS2 crystals creates a compressive stress around the glassy matrix.
- Very biocompatible - low plaque retention, adhesion and proliferation of human epithelial cells and gingival fibroblasts, especially when well polished.
- 30% more translucent than conventional zirconia

# Zirconia
- Chemical formula: ZrO2
- Classification - heterogenous polycrystalline ceramic
- Mechanical characteristics: toughness: 5–10 MPa√m, flexural strength: 500–1200 MPa, Young’s modulus: 210 GPa
- Good optical properties
