# Autonomic Nervous System
- controls visceral functions
	- arterial pressure, GI motility, GI secretion, urinary heart rate, pupillary dilation, etc.
- consist of sympathetic and parasympathetic nerve fibers
- synaptic neurotransmitters involved
	- acetylcholine
		- secreted by <font color="#f79646">cholinergic</font> fibers
		- binds to cholinergic receptors 
			1) muscarinic
				- location: CNS & PNS 
				- function: mediates innervation to visceral organs
			2) nicotinic receptors
				- location: CNS and neuromuscular junctions
	- norepinephrine - secreted by **adrenergic** fibers
- ALL preganglionic neurons are <font color="#f79646">Cholinergic</font> in both sympathetic and parasympethic pathways
- most Postganglionic sympathetic neurons are **adrenergic**
	- - exceptions: sweat glands and some blood vessels are <font color="#f79646">cholinergic</font>.
- almost all postganglionic parasympathic neurons are <font color="#f79646">cholinergic</font>.
