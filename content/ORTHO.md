MEDICAL HISTORY
- certain diseases - not for orthodontic treatment

ALLERGIES
- latex
- Nickel allergies (does your patient use necklace that isn't gold, resulting in a rash?)
- will need to use plastic materials

ASTHMA
- chronic use of the inhaler makes patient prone to radicular resorption
- check root length and monitor the length throughout the treatment through periodic panoramic xrays
- **if radicular resorption does appear, you stop the treatment; wait for 3 months**
	- why 3 months? once diagnosed, it takes 3 months to stop
- why? because there's a controlled inflammation of the PDL during ortho treatment and it takes 3 months to subside

Rheumatoid Arthriitis
- autoimmune disease that causes inflammation of synovial joints
- treatment modification: short ortho treatment bc the microtraumas will affect the TMJ; NO elastics, limited ortho treatment

Diabetes
- make sure patient is controlled; if not controlled, no ortho
- good hygiene
- the patient has microangiopathy; this affects the blood supply to the bone; so the movements should be very limited because the patient may develop necrosis; check for pulp vitality every single appointment

INFECTIVE ENDOCARDITIS
- prophylactic antibiotics; in any dental treatment that involves bleeding
- do not use metal braces/archwires; only invasiline

EPILEPSY
- ask if the patient has had an MRI, how often? because you may not be able to use metal due to MRI; must use plastic
- frequency of attacks?
- may have problems with gingival hyperplasia due to medication

LEUKEMIA
- if active ortho is diagnosed with cancer, stop ortho treatment and remove everything
- patient must wait for at least 3 months before chemotherapy because the ortho treatment produces inflammation of the PDL and the patient must not start chemotherapy if there is existing patients.  must inform oncologist

PSYCHIATRIC DISORDERS
- ADHD - children that can't or wont follow orders; must not start ortho treatment unless they are medicated; you must explain to the parents that if they do not follow the treatment/ or are noncompliant, the ortho treatment will be cancelled; like biting hard things, resulting in brackets being dislodged; when these kids grow and become adults, they develop OPPOSITIONAL DEFIAN DISORDER; cancel treatment
- OPPOSITIONAL DEFIANT DISORDER - not possible to diagnose during interview; you will only know once patient starts ortho treatment; they do not follow orders and have problems with authority; complain about everything; nothing is perfect; everything has a problem; you will place the brackets and chew everything and result in brackets falling off; cancel treatment
- DEPRESSION - any treatment that doesn't fulfill the patient's needs is a failure; if patient has depression, the patient will NEVER be satisfied; have the patient sign a consent form to avoid legal problems; explain nature of treatment and treatment results
