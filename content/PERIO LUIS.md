# Healing patttern of periodontal tissues
## first intention
-  join edges of the wound with sutures. First intention wounds are associated with minimal tissue loss and regeneration predominates over fibrosis
- closing an incision with sutures (e.g. impacted 3rd molar extraction)
	
## second intention
-  Surgical wounds that are allowed to heal without approximating the edges. Formation of granulation tissue and subsequent formation of epithelium on the granulation tissue. Scarring is evident as there is significant fibrosis.
	- e.g. simple extraction site
## third intention
- When there is extensive tissue loss, the wound must heal by contraction of the wound edges and the formation of granulation tissue. In some cases, the presence of a foreign body or infection may be suspected, and these wounds deliberately left open for several days until the potential complication resovles. When resolution has occurred, the edges of the wound can be brought together (approximated) and the wound proceeds to heal
- e.g. I&D marsupialization

## partial healing
- it occurs when a partial thickness wound closes primarily by epitheliazation. This wound healing involves the superficial portion of the dermis (lamina propria). There is minimal collagen deposition and absence of wound contraction.
- e.g. ulcers

# What is regenerative Surgery
- TEchniques designed to restore lost portions of the supporting structures of teeth, including root cementum PDL, and root bone
- Indications: Deep infrabony defects
- Furcation bone defects
- A - 1 wall defect: need to open buccal and lingual flap + **membrane (to avoid epithelial migration) + bone graft**
- B - 2 wall defect: just buccal flap; **CT + bone graft**
- C - 3 wall defect: **bone graft** only
- D - Just debride
- need stability of bone graft for success

# Guided tissue regeneration
- it involves the placement of a physical barrier membrane to allow the tooh root surface previously affected by periodontitis to repopulate with PDL cells, cementum cells and alveolar bone.
- corticotomy = osteopenia affect by placing some incisions between the teeth to increase mobility, diminish the probability of root resorption
- too much pressure during orthodontic movement on the root surface will activate osteoclasts located on the surface of the bone opposing the root surface, causing resorption.  

# Indications for periodontal regenerative surgery
- control of periodontal disease
- aesthetic considerations
- improve function
- improves long-term prognosis
