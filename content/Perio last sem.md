## Altered Passive Eruption
- Occurs when the teeth fully erupt but the gingival tissues fail to recede to the normal apical level
- The alveolar crest has a normal relationship with the CEJ
- The only abnormality is the coronal displacement of the gingival margin position
- Resulting in excessive gingival display and short clinical crowns

# Socket classification

- Facial bone and soft tissues are critical to decide the dimensional alterations
- Significant resorption of the facial bone in a horizontal and/or vertical direction occurs during the first 8-12 weeks
- Resorption is a physiologic healing response to the interruption of blood supply to the bone lining the extraction socket

# Early implant placement following osft tissue healing (Type 2)
- Tissue healing: between 4-8 weeks depending on the orofacial dimensions of the extracted tooth
- Main advantage is the increased amount of keratinized mucosa in the implant site
- healing creates 3-5mm of keratinized tissue
- bigger better amount of soft tissue
- keratinized tissue simplifies a tension-free primary closure of the flap = esthetics
- 4 weeks for lateral  incisors in the maxilla and premolars in both jaws
- 6-8 weeks for central incisors and canines in the maxilla
- during this time bone resorption is mainly limited to bundle bone
- To avoid extra resorption, the extraction must be done flapless, whenever possible
- interproximal bone after extraction is not reduced after 4-8 weeks
- Although flattening fo the facial bone is commonly observed in the middle of the extraction site
- Suitable technique fo ra 2-3 wall defect with the exposed implant surface contained within the alveolar crest
- the facial contour is always agumented with GBR
- Other advantage, the elimination of acute infections prior implant placrement
- Disadvantage: increased number of procedures
	- extraction'
	- implant placement with GBR
	- reopening procedure
