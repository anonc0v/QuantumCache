## How long does it take for epinephrine to go away after infiltration?
- epinephrine will act 5 min after you apply it but will remain in the body for 6-8 hours.
- so if patient experiences a little bit more bleeding (not profuse) after 6-8 hours it's because the epinephrine has been excreted.
