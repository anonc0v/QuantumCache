# Trigeminal Nerve
- largest of the 12 cranial nerves
- Sensory
	- tripartite, larger root
	- teeth, bone, soft tissues of the oral cavity
	- 3 branches
- Motor
	- smaller root
	- muscles of mastication

## Motor Root
- origin: **motor nucleus** within the pons and medulla oblongata
- separate from sensory root but travels in parallel, anteriorly towards the **semilunar (gasserian or trigeminal) ganglion**
- from the semilunar ganglion, motor root passes laterally and inferiorly towards the *foramen ovale*, through which it leaves the middle cranial fossa (together with the 3rd division of the sensory root - **mandibular nerve**)
- from the foramen ovale, the motor and sensory roots combine to form a single nerve trunk.

## Sensory Root
- Trigeminal ganglion innervates each side of the face (i.e. 2 ganglia total)
- location: Meckel cavity - anterior surface of petrous portion of temporal bone
- size: 1.0 x 2.0 cm; shape: crescent, convexities facing anteriorly and downward
- Sensory fibers enter from the concave side, forming the 3 sensory divisions of the trigeminal nerve

> [!note] Sensory Divisions of the Trigeminal Nerve
> 1) V1 - opthalmic division - **exits skull through SOF** (superior orbital fissure) into orbit
> 2) V2 - maxillary division - **exits skull through foramen rotundum**
> 3) V3 - mandibular division - **exits skull with motor root through foramen ovale**, the roots unite to form one nerve trunk that enters the *infratemporal fossa*


# Maxillary Nerve - V2
- Purely sensory
- exits skull through *foramen* *rotundum*, which is located in the greater wing of Sphenoid
- crosses the most superior part of the pterygopalatine fossa, between the pterygoid plates of sphenoid and palatine bone.  
- as it crosses, gives of branches to the sphenopalatine ganglion:
	- 1) posterior superior alveolar nerve
	- 2) zygomatic branches
- also gives branches to pterygopalatine ganglion
- enters orbit through inferior orbital fissure, occupying the infraorbital groove and becomes the infraorbital nerve, which courses anteriorly into the infraorbital canal.
- the maxillary division emerges from the infraorbital foramen anteriorly, where it divides into terminal branches
	- innervation: skin of face, nose, lower eyelid, and upper lip

# Mandibular Division V3

> [!note] Mandibular Division V3 of Trigeminal
> - Largest branch of Trigeminal Nerve
> - Mixed: Sensory + Motor
> - The only motor component of the trigeminal nerve
> - Origin of Sensory: Trigeminal Ganglion
> - Origin of Motor: Pons and Medulla Oblongota
