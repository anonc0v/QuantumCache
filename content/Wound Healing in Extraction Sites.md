- occurs by second intention
- blood immediately fills extraction site
- activation of intrinsic and extrinsic pathways of the clotting cascade >>> fibrin meshwork
- within 24-48 hours, **clot organization** begins:
	- engorgement and dilation of blood vessels within the PDL remnants
	- leukocytic migration
	- formation of fibrin layer
- 1st week
	- inflammatory cells migrate into the clot
	- epithlium grows over the surface of the organizing clot
	- accumulation of osteoclasts along the alveolar bone crest >>> crestal resorption
	- angiogenesis proceeds in the PDL remnants
- 2nd week:
	- continued clot organization through fibroplasia
	- new blood vessels penetrate toward the center of the clot
	- osteoid trabeculae extend into the clot from the alveolus
	- osteoclastic resorption of the cortical margin of the socket is more defined
	3rd week:
	- socket is filled with granulation tissue 
	- poorly calcified bone forms at the wound perimeter
	- wound surface is completely reepithelized with minimal or no scar formation
	- bone remodeling continues for several more weeks
	- reorganization of the alveolar site can continue up to 1 year after extraction BUT
	- most dimensional changes are complete in the first 3 months.
	- months to years: precursor woven bone remodels into lamellar bone and bone marrow

## Alveolar osteitis
- blood clot does not form or disintegrates
- without healthy granulation tissue, healing is delayed considerably
