# 🦷 Zirconia–Composite Bonding Notes (Provisional Restoration Using Panavia V5)

## 📌 Clinical Scenario
- Missing tooth **#7**
- Adjacent teeth **#6 and #8** have **zirconia crowns**
- Provisional pontic made of **bisacryl or composite resin**
- Bonded temporarily to zirconia for **6-week temporization**

## 🎯 Objective
To **securely attach** a resin pontic to **zirconia crowns** using a reliable bonding protocol that ensures:
- Adequate strength for 6 weeks
- Compatibility with **Panavia V5** system
- Use of **Tooth & Ceramic Primer**

## ⚙️ Materials Involved

| Material | Role |
|---------|------|
| **Zirconia crowns (#6, #8)** | Abutment support for pontic |
| **Composite pontic** (pre-cured or bisacryl) | Provisional restoration |
| **Panavia V5** | Resin cement |
| **Tooth & Ceramic Primer (Kuraray)** | MDP + Silane dual primer |
| **Air abrasion (Al₂O₃)** | Surface roughening |
| **Ribbond fiber** | Reinforcement across pontic |

## 🧪 Primer Chemistry Breakdown

| Component | Bonds To | Bond Type |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **MDP (10-Methacryloyloxydecyl dihydrogen phosphate)** | **Zirconia** | **P–O–Zr (chemical)** + hydrogen bonds |
| **Silane (e.g., 3-MPS)** | **Silica-based composite fillers** | **Si–O–Si** covalent bonds |
| **Methacrylate group** | **Resin cement** | Polymerization |

## 🔗 Visualized Bonding Chain

```
Zirconia (ZrO₂ surface)
     ↓
P–O–Zr bond (via MDP phosphate)
     ↓
Methacrylate end of MDP → co-polymerizes with resin cement
     ↑
Resin cement matrix
     ↑
Silane → bonds to exposed silica in composite pontic
     ↑
Pre-cured composite pontic (if surface prepped)
```

## 🧰 Bonding Protocol (Step-by-Step)

### 🟡 On Zirconia Crowns (#6, #8)

1. **Air abrade** with 30–50 μm Al₂O₃ at ~2 bar
2. **Clean** with ethanol or Ivoclean (optional but improves reliability)
3. **Apply Tooth & Ceramic Primer**
   - Wait 20 sec
   - Air dry
4. Do **not etch zirconia** (etching ineffective)

### 🔵 On Composite Pontic (pre-cured)

1. **Roughen** surface (fine diamond, sandblast, or microetch)
2. **Clean** surface (e.g., with alcohol)
3. **Apply Tooth & Ceramic Primer**
   - Silane bonds to exposed silica
4. **No etching** required on pre-cured composite

### 🟢 Bonding with Panavia V5

1. Mix and apply Panavia V5 cement
2. Seat pontic against zirconia crowns
3. Light cure margins (~2–3 sec)
4. Remove excess and then final cure
5. Finish/polish margins as needed

## 📏 Thickness & Design Considerations

- **Rest seat depth:** 1.0–1.5 mm
- **Connector thickness (resin):** ≥1.5–2.0 mm for strength
- **Contact area of pontic with zirconia:** maximize surface to increase bond stability
- **No need to reduce contact points** if thickness is achieved elsewhere on pontic base

## 🧰 Reinforcement with Ribbond

- Use **Ribbond polyethylene fiber** embedded within composite
  - Improves **flexural strength and crack resistance**
  - Lightweight, easy to handle, and compatible with bonding agents
  - Ideal for provisional bridge reinforcement
  - Must be saturated with adhesive and fully encapsulated in composite resin
